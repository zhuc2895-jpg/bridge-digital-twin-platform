# Fusion Enhancement Boundaries

Model cognition, spatial residual arbitration, simulation-measurement compensation, and modal deformation inversion are separate evidence sources rather than one opaque score. GAN-AE exposes reconstruction-error mean/variance and cognition; low cognition or high uncertainty caps a confirmed vote at yellow suspected and routes it through feature arbitration. Cross-sensor ridge residuals distinguish an isolated channel from coherent multi-sensor change, capping only the isolated case. Compensation returns a prediction only when the simulation prior and measurement correction are in-domain and reliable; otherwise it returns the physical-threshold fallback reason. Modal inversion selects modes by energy, filters low-quality directions, solves weighted ridge coordinates, and reports reconstruction and full-field uncertainty.

The VBA acceptance artifact keeps the frozen raw alert-pipeline baseline and the enhanced fusion result separate. Raw any-alert metrics and gated/suppressed counts remain visible so improved classification cannot be manufactured by silently deleting evidence. The enhanced classifier is supervised only on out-of-fold recording labels; it is not presented as an unsupervised online detector. Compensation and modal checks use synthetic fixtures and are explicitly labeled as contract/behavior validation, not real bridge accuracy.

## Consequences

- A low-confidence AI result may still be useful, but it cannot directly claim orange or red severity.
- Spatial evidence can suppress isolated noise without suppressing coherent structural changes.
- Response estimation fails closed to physical thresholds when uncertainty or domain support is insufficient.
- Acceptance reports must state whether a metric came from VBA recordings or from synthetic fixtures.
